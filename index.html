rclone-oyxc

rclone-oyxc is a private Google OAuth client used only by its owner, Jayson_O, to run rclone for personal backups of his own Google Drive.

It is not a public service. It is not offered to other users, and it is not used for any commercial purpose.

The client requests read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly). Files are downloaded to the owner's personal home server as a backup copy. Nothing is uploaded, changed or deleted in Google Drive.
